# Recommended Future Research Directions

**Project:** EcoSentinel  
**Date:** 2026-11-22  
**Status:** Recommendations only. No change to research question, dataset, Gate status, or protocol. Gate 2 remains **PAUSED / NOT APPROVED — Option C (Exploratory only)**.

## 1. Prioritization principles

Recommendations are prioritized by: (1) resolving Gate 2 evidence blocker, (2) strengthening evaluation credibility, (3) reducing confounding, (4) only then exploring method sophistication. All require human approval where they involve external contact, data access, protocol decisions, or scope changes.

## 2. Recommended directions

| Priority | Direction | Why | Feasibility | Dependencies / approvals | Risk if skipped |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P0** | **Obtain/approve independent biological labels and exposure evidence** | Gate 2 blocker; without this, no confirmatory evaluation. | Medium-low (depends on data access/authorization) | Human authorization for data requests; data licensing/provenance review; Gate 2 adjudication. | Project remains exploratory; no defensible performance claim. |
| **P1** | **Full-text comparator review** (Recknagel 2025, Rousso 2020 included studies, other high-frequency HAB EWS) | Prevent false originality claims; sharpen contribution. | High (desk research) | None beyond time; follow citation chains. | Overclaiming novelty; weak scientific positioning. |
| **P1** | **Define event/non-event adjudication protocol (human-approved)** | Needed before any real evaluation. | Medium | Human scientific decision; independent evidence. | Circular/invalid evaluation. |
| **P1** | **Build matched-FAR baseline suite on synthetic/approved data** | Establish whether any relationship-change indicator beats simple baselines. | High (software) | Approved labels or synthetic-only framing. | Complex method appears good only because baselines missing. |
| **P2** | **Autocorrelation/seasonality null-model study** | Rolling correlations highly vulnerable to spurious signals. | Medium | Synthetic + real predictor data; no biological labels needed for null behavior. | False positives interpreted as ecological signals. |
| **P2** | **Hydrology/management stratification analysis** | Painter 2023 shows flow/source-water management affects Buffalo Pound blooms. | Medium | Contextual data integration; careful interpretation. | Confounded attribution. |
| **P2** | **Sensor-artefact negative controls** | Fouling/calibration can mimic signals. | Medium | Quality flags, maintenance metadata if available. | Artefact-driven alerts. |
| **P3** | **Compare relationship-change indicators to CSD indicators** | Tests whether generic EWS or specific coupling-change is informative. | Medium | Same evaluation constraints. | Miss simpler/stronger alternative. |
| **P3** | **Event-type stratification** (Dolichospermum vs Planktothrix etc.) | Painter 2022 shows different bloom phases/species. | Low until labels exist | Independent taxonomy/biovolume labels. | One-size-fits-all signal misses biology. |
| **P4** | **Cross-site transferability study** | Tests generalizability hypothesis. | Low | External datasets, harmonization, approvals. | Overgeneralized claims. |

## 3. What not to do next

- Do not train or tune predictive models on proxy labels without independence/circularity safeguards.
- Do not resume Gate 2/4 based on literature plausibility alone.
- Do not claim "early warning" without matched-FAR and independent-event evaluation.
- Do not generalize to other ecosystems without evidence.

## 4. Minimum evidence package to strengthen the project

1. Independent biological event records (taxonomy/biovolume/toxin with provenance), or explicitly approved alternative.
2. Defensible non-event exposure definition.
3. Full-text comparator matrix against closest studies.
4. Pre-registered evaluation protocol (human-approved).
5. Baseline comparison including univariate thresholds and simple multivariate anomaly scores.
6. Uncertainty reporting and negative controls.

## 5. Decision gate

Only after P0/P1 evidence and protocol decisions are human-approved should the project consider confirmatory model evaluation. Until then, EcoSentinel remains an exploratory research program with an untested hypothesis.
