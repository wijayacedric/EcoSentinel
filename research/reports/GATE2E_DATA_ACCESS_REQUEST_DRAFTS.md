## Why the requested data matters scientifically

| Data type | Why it matters for EcoSentinel | How it would be used |
|---|---|---|
| Phytoplankton taxonomy/enumeration/cell counts | Identifies cyanobacterial taxa and abundance independently of buoy RFU sensors | Candidate biological bloom-event reconstruction under pre-approved criteria |
| Cyanobacterial/phytoplankton biomass or biovolume | Quantifies biological magnitude and dominance; supports interval-censored event assessment | Assess whether observations meet defensible biological criteria; avoid sensor-only labels |
| Chlorophyll-a laboratory measurements | Independent lab pigment measurement (not buoy RFU) | Context/corroboration only; not used as sole ground truth when circular |
| Microcystin/other cyanotoxin measurements | Independent toxin evidence | Corroborate toxin presence/absence; cannot replace taxonomy/biovolume |
| Sample dates/times/locations/depths | Needed to align observations to buoy record and assess spatial relevance | Temporal alignment, interval censoring, spatial representativeness assessment |
| Methods, units, detection limits, QA/QC, sample IDs/replicates | Needed for reproducibility, comparability, and uncertainty assessment | Data verification, anomaly investigation, uncertainty documentation |
| Buoy-informed phase boundary metadata (if any) | Needed to avoid circular ground truth | Separate independent biological measurements from buoy-informed interpretations |
