## 2. Minimum conditions for Gate 2 approval

Gate 2 should remain unapproved unless all are satisfied:

1. **Independent biological evidence:** record-level, method-documented, provenance-tracked biological observations independent of buoy RFU predictors used for evaluation.
2. **Event definition:** pre-approved biological-only event criteria, including thresholds/qualifiers/taxon/toxin rules as applicable.
3. **Interval censoring:** pre-approved handling of sampling gaps, onset/termination uncertainty, and unresolved anomalies.
4. **Non-event exposure:** defensible verified exposure denominator; unsampled/missing periods never treated as non-bloom.
5. **Event count/power:** sufficient independently reconstructed events and exposure for meaningful confirmatory evaluation; uncertainty reported.
6. **Evaluation design:** temporal/blocked validation, leakage controls, baselines, matched-FAR primary comparison, missed-event and lead-time bounds.
7. **Human review:** ChatGPT/human review and explicit Gate 2 approval recorded.
