Can the current Buffalo Pound Lake dataset, combined with independently documented biological observations, support a defensible evaluation of the research question?

## 3. Outcome assessment

### A. Feasible with existing evidence — **NO**
**Reason:** Existing accessible evidence cannot support a defensible confirmatory evaluation. No independently reconstructed biological bloom events; no defensible non-event exposure; no approved event/non-event criteria; matched FAR and missed-event rates cannot be credibly estimated.

**Evidence supporting A is insufficient:**
- Painter 2022 provides 42 discrete point observations over 2018-2019, not approved event intervals.
- Boyer 2017 raw data not accessible.
- BPWTP microcystin is sparse toxin-only evidence.
- 2014-2016 and 2020-2021 lack verified independent phytoplankton/biovolume records.

**What would invalidate A:** N/A - A is not met.

### B. Conditionally feasible — **PLAUSIBLE BUT UNPROVEN**
**Reason:** The study could become testable only if specific missing records/metadata are obtained and human-approved biological-only criteria are established before any reconstruction.

**Required for B:**
1. Accessible record-level independent biological data covering missing years and/or filling key gaps (Boyer 2017 raw/SI; BPWTP/WSA phytoplankton taxonomy/cell counts/biovolume/lab chl-a/cyanotoxins with methods/QA).
2. Pre-approved biological-only event definition, interval-censoring rules, and defensible non-event exposure rules (human/ChatGPT decision).
3. Sufficient independent events and verified exposure to estimate matched FAR, missed-event rate, and lead-time bounds with stated uncertainty.
4. Independence from buoy predictors confirmed for labels; pigment RFU not used as ground truth.

**Conditions that would invalidate B:**
- Records do not exist, are inaccessible, or lack required metadata/QA.
- Sampling resolution remains too sparse for defensible onset/termination/exposure.
- Obtained records reveal circularity, inconsistency, or insufficient event count.
- Approved criteria yield too few evaluable events/non-event exposure for meaningful confirmatory evaluation.

### C. Not currently feasible — **YES, FOR CONFIRMATORY EVALUATION NOW**
**Reason:** With currently accessible evidence, the research question cannot be confirmatorily tested. This is a present-state conclusion, not a claim that records do not exist elsewhere.

**Conditions that would invalidate C:**
- Human-authorized acquisition of sufficient independent biological records with required metadata.
- Human-approved Gate 2/3 criteria.
- Successful independent event/exposure reconstruction demonstrating adequate evaluable sample.

## 4. Final feasibility classification

| Dimension | Classification | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmatory evaluation (current accessible evidence) | **C — Not currently feasible** | 0 confirmed events; 0 defensible exposure; no approved criteria |
| Exploratory/descriptive work | **Allowed under Option C** | With strict safeguards, no labels, no confirmatory claims |
| Potential future confirmatory feasibility | **B — Conditionally feasible** | Only if required records/metadata obtained and human-approved criteria established |

## 5. Strongest evidence supporting conclusion

- Gate 2 evidence register and Gate 2D/2E/2F/2G reports consistently show 0 independently reconstructed biological bloom events.
- Painter 2022 is the strongest accessible independent biological dataset but is sparse/interval-censored and has unresolved 2019-07-17 anomaly.
- Boyer 2017 raw record-level data not accessible; published phase boundaries partly buoy-informed.
- BPWTP provides toxin-only sparse intake data, not taxonomy/biovolume event reconstruction.
- No defensible verified non-bloom exposure denominator exists.

## 6. Largest remaining obstacle

**Absence of accessible, record-level, independent biological observations with sufficient temporal coverage/metadata to reconstruct events and defensible non-event exposure under approved criteria.**

## 7. Single most valuable next action

**Obtain (with human authorization) record-level 2014–2021 independent phytoplankton/taxonomy/biovolume/cyanotoxin/lab chl-a records with dates, location/depth, methods, units, QA, and detection limits from BPWTP/WSA and/or Boyer/Painter source data custodians; then perform human-reviewed Gate 2 adjudication.** No follow-up should be sent without explicit human authorization.
