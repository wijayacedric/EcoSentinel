## 2. Eligibility and entry requirements

From the provisional 2027 student rules:

- Open to second-level students **born after 1 May 2008**, attending school or home-schooled in Ireland (including Northern Ireland).
- A student may enter **one project only**, at **one venue only**.
- Categories include **Life Sciences**, which explicitly includes environmental science.
- Each project needs a separate entry form.
- The student’s teacher must be registered on the online entry system and must confirm the entry.
- A signed Participation Consent and Media Release form is required; a parent/guardian must also sign if under 18.
- Students must bring a **Project Report Book** and a **Visual Display**.
- Work carried out in an institution or under a supervisor other than the teacher must be acknowledged in the report book, with name and contact details.
- Continuation projects are allowed if they document additional research; simply repeating the same methodology is not acceptable.
- Dangerous equipment, pathogenic/toxigenic organisms, and live mammals/birds/reptiles are not allowed at the exhibition.

**Personal eligibility:** The user’s date of birth is not recorded in the repository. The “born after 1 May 2008” condition cannot be verified here; the user must confirm it.

## 4. Stage 1 feasibility

### 4.1 What must happen

| Step | Earliest realistic completion | Dependency |
|---|---|---|
| 4L.14 review addendum | 2026-12-10 | This package; human review |
| Scientific/statistical/data-custodian review | 2026-12-10 to 2027-01-15 | Human reviewers |

| Analysis and figures | 2027-03-15 | D-15 authorisation |
| Entry form | 2027-03-11 | Teacher registration; project description |
| Report book and display | By venue fair (April–May) | Analysis, writing, design |
| Fair | April–May 2027 | Venue date |

### 4.2 Assessment

**Feasible, but tight.** The critical path is governance, not analysis. If the addendum is reviewed and D-01–D-14 are recorded by mid-January, a count-only audit and a descriptive analysis can be completed before the March entry and April/May fair. If approvals slip past mid-February, the project can still be entered, but the analysis and display will be rushed.

**Strengths:**

- Data are already local and verified.
- The analysis is descriptive and computationally light.
- The protocol and decision register already exist.
- No new access, equipment, or fieldwork is required.

**Risks:**

- D-15 and the count audit are separate approvals; conflating them would violate governance.
- The actual *n* may be too small after QC, forcing a “points only” or “descriptive, not stable” outcome.
- The 4L.14 review findings on coverage, duplicates, and taxon absence are not yet resolved; they may change the analysis population.
- The licence/competition review (D-14) is incomplete.
- Student ownership and presentation quality are not established by documents.

### 4.3 Verdict

**Stage 1 is feasible for SciFest 2027 if governance is unblocked by mid-January 2027.** It is not feasible to add a new field season or a new external dataset as the primary project.

## 5. Field study feasibility

A student-led field study would require:

- a field site and access;
- a boat or sampling platform;
- a sonde/fluorometer and calibration;
- microscopy laboratory and taxonomic expertise;
- permits/ethics and supervision;
- a full ice-free season for sampling;
- lab and analysis time.

The 2027 fair season runs **April–May 2027**. A summer 2027 field season begins **after** the fairs. Even a May field start cannot produce a completed analysis before an April/May fair. The field study is therefore **not a viable primary project for SciFest 2027** unless fieldwork was already underway before this assessment, which is not documented.

It could become viable for a later competition year if the field season is completed in 2027 and analysed for 2028.

## 6. Stage 2/Müggelsee feasibility

Even if IGB grants access immediately:

- the data are large and split into multiple blocks;
- the biological and sensor records need a metadata/data-dictionary audit;
- bloom events must be independently defined and counted;
- the spatial/depth mismatch must be assessed;
- sensor era changes and QC must be documented;
- a Stage 2 protocol and human approvals would be required.

This cannot credibly be the primary SciFest 2027 project. It is a **future Stage 2** path. It could inform a later competition or a long-term research programme.
