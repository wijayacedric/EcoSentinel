# Option A / B / C Comparison

**Date:** 2026-11-25  
**Purpose:** Independently compare the three strategic options. This document does not approve any option.

---

## 1. The options

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **A** | Continue the narrowed two-lake measurement study as the entire competition project. |
| **B** | Pivot to the student-led phycocyanin–biovolume field study. |
| **C** | Adopt a sequential programme: Stage 1 narrowed measurement study now; Stage 2 original early-warning question only if a qualifying dataset is acquired. |

## 3. Independent challenge to Option C

Option C is not automatically the best choice. Its weaknesses are real:

1. **It can become “two projects.”** Stage 1 and Stage 2 use different data, different questions, and different timelines. If Stage 2 is pursued before Stage 1 is finished, the student may end up with two incomplete projects.
2. **Stage 1 may be too incremental.** The core question — does fluorescence rank-track microscopy biomass? — is already studied. A judge may ask why this is a new contribution rather than a local reanalysis.
3. **Stage 2 may never happen.** Müggelsee access is request-only; WSA has not replied; a qualifying dataset may not arrive. The “sequential” path could become a permanent deferral.
4. **The original question remains untested.** If the competition requires an early-warning result, Option C does not deliver one in 2027.

These are reasons to adopt C **with a hard gate**, not reasons to reject it:

- Stage 1 must be completed and presented as a bounded measurement study, not as a preliminary exercise.
- Stage 2 must have a dated go/no-go decision and a real dataset, not an aspiration.
- If Stage 2 data do not arrive by the gate, the project stays with Stage 1 and does not pretend otherwise.

---

## 4. Does Stage 1 have enough scientific value?

**Yes, but modestly.**

Stage 1 can deliver:

- a transparent two-lake comparison of surface fluorescence and independent microscopy abundance;
- a documented negative/limited result about proxy performance;
- a data-quality and depth-alignment audit that is useful to other monitoring studies;
- a clear specification of what would be needed for early warning.

It cannot deliver:

- a new early-warning method;
- a validated sensor calibration;
- a general cross-lake result;
- a causal depth effect;
- a bloom forecast.

The scientific value is therefore **educational and methodological**, not a breakthrough. That is enough for a defensible school/regional project if framed honestly. It is not enough to justify overclaiming.

It is **not merely a preliminary exercise** if the negative/limited result is treated as the contribution: a documented demonstration that surface RFU proxies diverge from depth-integrated microscopy, plus an explicit data-sufficiency analysis, is a legitimate finding. It **is** merely preliminary if it is presented as a warm-up for an early-warning study that never happens, or if the result is used only to justify a future dataset request.

---

## 5. Could the field study strengthen Stage 1?

Potentially, in one specific way: a small, local paired sampling campaign could provide a **calibration subset** that Stage 1 lacks. But:

- it would need to be designed around the same sensors and endpoints;
- it would need replication and taxonomic support;
- it would add a second research project and a field season;
- it would not be ready for the 2027 fair.

It is therefore a **Stage 1b add-on for a later year**, not a component of the 2027 project.

## 6. Decision matrix

| Option | Recommendation | Confidence | Main reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — Narrowed only | Accept as Stage 1, not as the whole programme | High | Feasible and defensible; preserves the original question only as an aspiration |
| B — Field study pivot | Reject as primary | High | Not early warning; not feasible for 2027; incremental literature |
| C — Sequential | **Adopt with a hard Stage 2 gate** | Moderate–high | Best balance of feasibility, honesty, and preservation of the original ambition |

**Primary recommendation: C.** Stage 1 is the current project; Stage 2 is a conditional research path, not a promise.
