### Why Stage 1 is worth pursuing

Stage 1 can deliver:

- a transparent two-lake comparison of surface fluorescence and independent microscopy abundance;
- a documented negative/limited result about proxy performance;
- a data-quality and depth-alignment audit;
- a clear specification of what would be needed for early warning.

It cannot deliver:

- a new early-warning method;
- a validated sensor calibration;
- a general cross-lake result;
- a causal depth effect;
- a bloom forecast.

Its value is **methodological and educational**, not a breakthrough. That is enough for a defensible school/regional project if framed honestly. It is not enough for a top-tier original-research claim.

### Why the field study is not the primary pivot

- It tests calibration/community composition, not early warning.
- It is incremental relative to Bastien, Cotterill, Thomson-Laing, Ma, and Painter.
- It needs a field season, sonde, microscopy lab, taxonomist, permits, and supervision.
- A summer 2027 field season is after the April–May 2027 fairs.
- It would create a second research project rather than finish the current one.

It could be a **Stage 1b add-on** only if fully resourced and tightly scoped, or a **future-year project**.

### Why Option C is conditional

Option C is not automatically correct. It risks becoming two incomplete projects or a permanent deferral. It is recommended only with:

- a hard Stage 2 go/no-go date — recommended **31 January 2027**, after the Müggelsee/WSA enquiries and before SciFest entry;
- a qualifying dataset, not an aspiration;
- Stage 1 completed and presented as a bounded measurement study;
- no overclaiming while Stage 2 is pending.

## 4. Stage 2 criteria and Müggelsee

The six proposed criteria are necessary but insufficient. They should be expanded to include:

- access/licence;
- independent biological endpoint;
- cyanobacteria-specific taxonomy;
- time-stamped sampling;
- cadence tied to the warning horizon;
- multi-year overlap;
- ≥10 independent events;
- non-bloom exposure;
- spatial/depth representativeness;
- sensor QC/calibration;
- **pre-specified independent event definition**;
- data structure/provenance;
- practical intake feasibility.

**Müggelsee verdict:** credible lead, not qualified.

It passes duration, cadence, and biological-endpoint criteria at metadata level. It fails or remains unresolved on access, strict co-location, event definition, and QC/calibration. The authorised email has been sent; access is awaited.
