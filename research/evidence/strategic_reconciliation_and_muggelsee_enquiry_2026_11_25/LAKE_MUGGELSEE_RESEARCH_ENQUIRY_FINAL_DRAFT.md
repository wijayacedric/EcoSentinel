## 4. Final email body

Dear Dr Hilt and Dr Shatwell,

My name is Cedric Wijaya. I am a secondary-school student in Ireland, developing an independent freshwater ecology research project called EcoSentinel.

EcoSentinel investigates whether persistent changes in relationships between freshwater environmental variables can provide earlier warning of cyanobacterial blooms than conventional monitoring, at comparable false-alarm rates. The project is currently completing a limited two-lake measurement/comparability study using existing data. Its longer-term objective is to investigate early-warning performance using suitable long-term biological and high-frequency environmental records.

I have been studying the IGB FRED metadata for Lake Müggelsee, particularly the long-term phytoplankton dataset (FRED 867), the weekly multiparameter probe (FRED 1), and the high-frequency station (FRED 1145). The combination of a long weekly/biweekly phytoplankton series, species-level biovolume and biomass, and high-frequency phycocyanin, chlorophyll, temperature and oxygen records appears potentially relevant to that longer-term objective. I am not assuming that the records are suitable, and I would be grateful for your guidance on whether they could support an independent student analysis and what limitations I should understand.

I would particularly like to ask about four areas.

Biological records. Is the sampling frequency and station/depth integration correctly described as weekly during the growing season and biweekly under ice? Are cyanobacteria-specific taxonomic records, biovolume, biomass or cell-density measurements available at sample level? Are sampling dates and times available? Can bloom and non-bloom periods be identified independently from the biological records, and are non-bloom periods adequately represented?

Environmental and sensor records. I understand that the high-frequency station measures at 1.5 m and that phycocyanin measurements began around July 2007. Which variables are available in each period, and what are the main gaps? How do the high-frequency station and the phytoplankton sampling stations relate spatially? Are the integrated phytoplankton samples comparable with the fixed 1.5 m sensor, or would the profile data (FRED 165) be needed to address the depth difference?

Measurement quality. Are calibration and maintenance records available? How should the instrument changes — particularly 2007, 2014 and 2019 — be handled when comparing across years? Are quality-control flags and missing-data documentation included with the data? Are the sensor measurements suitable for comparisons across instruments and years?

Access and permissions. Could the relevant datasets be accessed for independent educational research? Does access require a formal request or institutional permission, and what licensing or citation requirements apply? Is a data dictionary or supporting documentation available? If you are not the right custodian for a particular dataset, could you recommend the appropriate person?

I am not asking you to conduct the project or to assess my hypothesis. I am trying to establish whether the data are suitable and accessible before making a formal data request. If a short metadata summary or data dictionary is available, that would be very helpful. I would also be glad to send a one-page summary of the project or to arrange a brief call if that is easier.

Thank you for your time, and for maintaining such a valuable long-term record.

Kind regards,

Cedric Wijaya  
EcoSentinel — independent student research project
