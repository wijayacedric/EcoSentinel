# Gate 2 Requirements and Blockers

**Gate status remains: PAUSED / NOT APPROVED — Option C (Exploratory Only).** The checklist below classifies whether the scientific-literature review supports readiness; it does not authorize or replace the formal Gate 2 review.

| Gate requirement | Status | Evidence and limitation | Evidence needed to clear or disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mendota has an independent biological monitoring record suitable in principle | **Conditionally supported** | NTL core catalog lists Madison phytoplankton from 1995-current; published Mendota studies report taxonomic microscopy, cell density and biovolume. Exact 88.31 fields and method history were not inspected here. | Authenticated schema/data inventory, units, taxa, dates, method/version history and QC. |
| Cyanobacteria resolved to useful taxonomic level | **Conditionally supported** | Hansen & Carey 2015 report species-level identification of >400 natural units/sample for the 1995–2011 subset; exclusions were made for unresolved species. Package-wide resolution may vary. | Confirm version 88.31 taxonomic fields, synonyms, unresolved categories and analyst/protocol changes. |
| Quantitative endpoint available | **Conditionally supported** | Published work reports cell densities and calculated biovolume; biomass by mass is not established. | Verify 88.31 fields, formulas and units; select one independent and justified abundance metric. |
| A bloom event can be independently adjudicated | **Unresolved** | Microscopy can measure abundance at sample dates; no package-wide event labels or independently justified threshold established. Taxon presence is not bloom status. | Endpoint/event rule supported by independent ecology/management source, with no sensor-proxy leakage. |
| Non-bloom exposure is measurable | **Unresolved** | Unequal snapshots cannot certify unsampled intervals; low sampled abundance may be useful only at visit times and with detection/effort known. | Observation-effort, detection and at-risk exposure rules; conservatively account for unsampled time and seasons. |
| Onset can be resolved for claimed horizon | **Conditionally supported** for multi-week horizon; **unsupported** for short daily/hourly onset claim from microscopy alone | Published sampling generally biweekly in summer and monthly non-summer. Onsets will be interval-censored; short events may be missed. | Actual interval-width audit and horizon-specific feasibility assessment after authorized event adjudication. |
| Compatible predictor series exists | **Conditionally supported** | High-frequency temperature, DO, pigments and meteorology documented; sonde profiles 2017-current. Exact common support, variables and valid data not analyzed. | Common support table from authorized package data and QC metadata. |
| Predictors are independent of biological reference | **Conditionally supported** | Microscopy is a distinct measurement process from buoy sensors. Pigment predictors must not define the endpoint; biology is not statistically independent of ecological-state predictors. | Endpoint adjudication blind to sensors and explicit predictor/endpoint separation. |
| Sensor depth/location represent biology | **Unresolved** | Deep-hole 0–8m integrated biological sample vs point sensor; sensor depth differs across sources (1.0m paper; 0.5m EML referenced in sensor audit). Single station cannot represent whole lake. | Resolve period-specific depths, coordinates and water-column representativeness; limit claim to paired site if needed. |
| Sensor time series homogeneous / quality controlled | **Unsupported as a long homogeneous series** | Multiple instrument eras and 2019 pigment scale discontinuity; documented 2017 outage/biofouling; inventory has year labels, not uptime/QC. | Sensor maintenance, cross-calibration, QC flags, exact deployment/transition dates, missingness audit; era restrictions if unresolved. |
| Comparable conventional baselines can be specified | **Conditionally supported** | Pigment and single-variable measures exist; measurement-era differences and target distinctions complicate fair comparisons. | Prespecify baseline(s), common predictor availability, era handling, and training-only thresholds. |
| Matched false-alarm evaluation is well-defined | **Unresolved** | Requires a common endpoint, origins, horizon, exposure denominator, alarm deduplication, and threshold selection. “Matched rate” alone is not sufficient. | Freeze common operational definitions and held-out thresholding procedure. |
| Temporal leakage can be controlled | **Supported in principle; unresolved operationally** | Whole-year/block holdout and past-only rolling calculations are feasible; no implementation audited here. | Frozen time-respecting split and training-only preprocessing/selection protocol. |
| Adequate independent events/exposure and uncertainty | **Unresolved** | No counts produced; high-frequency samples do not create independent bloom events. No arbitrary event-count rule applied. | Blinded endpoint feasibility census, interval widths, years, event-level precision/power analysis under proposed estimand. |
| Cross-lake generalization | **Unsupported by Mendota alone** | One lake cannot validate generalization to other freshwater systems. | Separate multi-lake, harmonized design with held-out lake; otherwise limit inference to Mendota. |
| Defensible contribution beyond prior art | **Conditionally supported** | Strong overlap with Mendota high-frequency pigment analyses, seasonal/subseasonal and continuous forecasting, and general multivariate lake EWS comparisons. Exact proposed detector has not been specified and exact novelty remains unresolved. | Method-by-method novelty matrix against full bibliography and explicit distinction of estimand, endpoint, horizon and operating-point test. |

## Gate recommendation

**Do not advance to confirmatory Gate 2 on this literature review alone. Retain Mendota for exploratory work and further evidence verification.** A readiness review could be justified only after material blockers above are resolved, especially (1) exact biological endpoint and sampled exposure, (2) onset interval widths versus horizon, (3) event and non-event support after QC/era restrictions, and (4) novel contribution under exact-method literature comparison. If the project cannot independently define bloom events or valid negatives, it should not describe a sensor proxy as validated microscopy-ground-truth early warning.
