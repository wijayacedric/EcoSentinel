## 6. Is Stage 1 scientifically defensible?

**Yes, conditionally — as a modest exploratory measurement/comparability study.**

It is defensible if:

- the question remains same-calendar-date rank association, not agreement/validation;
- the target population is explicitly detection-conditioned;
- P3/P4 remain exploratory until duplicate/biomass issues are resolved;
- the fixed 72/144 coverage rule stays withdrawn;
- negative/above-range RFU are treated as unresolved, not biological zeros;
- the two lakes remain separate case studies;
- all four estimates are reported as equal-status exploratory;
- no depth, lake, sensor, or calibration effect is claimed.

It is **not** defensible as:

- an early-warning validation;
- a sensor validation or calibration;
- a predictive study;
- a causal depth comparison;
- a general cross-lake result.

**Scientific value:** modest. It can produce a useful local negative/limited result and a transparent data-quality audit. It is not a breakthrough, but it is enough for a defensible school/regional project if framed honestly.
