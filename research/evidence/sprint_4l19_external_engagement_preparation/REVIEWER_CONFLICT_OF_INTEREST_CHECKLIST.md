### Guidance for the project owner

- A reviewer who is a **current author or data custodian** of the dataset should not review the study as an independent reviewer. They may be consulted as a custodian, but that is a different role.
- A reviewer with a **recent co-authorship** (within five years) should be treated as conflicted unless the review is limited to a part of the design unaffected by that relationship.
- A reviewer with a **general familiarity** with the lake or network, but no direct role in the dataset, may proceed with disclosure.
- A reviewer who **refuses to disclose** should not be appointed.
- Record the decision even when the conflict is minor.
