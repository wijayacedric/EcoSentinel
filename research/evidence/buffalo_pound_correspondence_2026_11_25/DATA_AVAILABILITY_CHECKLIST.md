# Buffalo Pound Data-Availability Checklist

**Date:** 2026-11-25  
**Status:** Documentation only — no data request beyond the existing correspondence  
**Gate 2:** PAUSED / NOT APPROVED — Option C (Exploratory Only)

## 1. Purpose

Use this checklist to assess whether any additional Buffalo Pound source can support the narrowed measurement study, before requesting or ingesting data.

A source is **not** useful merely because it is long or high-frequency. It must supply an independent biological endpoint that can be paired with sensor observations at a defensible temporal resolution.

## 2. Minimum useful endpoint

For P3/P4 primary/conditional use, the source should provide:

- [ ] cyanobacteria **cell counts** or **biovolume/biomass**;
- [ ] sample **date** and, ideally, **time**;
- [ ] sample **depth**;
- [ ] **taxonomic resolution** sufficient to identify cyanobacteria (species, genus, or a documented cyanobacteria group);
- [ ] **method** (microscopy, Utermöhl, flow imaging, etc.);
- [ ] **units** and **detection limits**;
- [ ] **QA/QC** metadata and sample identifiers;
- [ ] repeated observations across **more than two seasons/years**, ideally including non-bloom periods;
- [ ] access terms that permit educational/research use.

## 3. Source-specific questions

### A. Saskatchewan Water Security Agency — Heather Sauer

- Do the “some cell counts” include species-level taxonomy and cyanobacteria biovolume/biomass?
- Are sample date, time, location and depth recorded?
- Are laboratory methods, units, detection limits and QA/QC metadata available?
- What is the actual temporal coverage and cadence per site/year?
- Are there non-bloom/winter/spring observations?
- Is there a metadata summary or data dictionary that can be shared before full data?
- What are the access and citation conditions?

### B. University of Regina — Peter Leavitt

- Are the chlorophyll and HPLC pigment series archived at the same biweekly dates?
- Do the HPLC pigments include cyanobacteria-specific markers (e.g., zeaxanthin, echinenone) that could support a functional-group comparison?
- Is there a data dictionary and documented method?
- Are sample depth and time recorded?
- What are the access terms?
- Can the data be used as **contextual** only, given no cell counts?

### C. City of Regina Water Treatment Plant — Helen Baulch

- What phytoplankton composition data exist, at what taxonomic resolution?
- Are cell counts or biovolume/biomass available, or only presence/approximate composition?
- Is chlorophyll available, and by what method?
- What are the sample dates, depths and methods?
- What are the access terms?
- Is the plant data independent of the buoy/sensor data, or derived from the same monitoring?

### D. University of Saskatchewan — Helen Baulch

- Do the high-resolution sensor deployments have paired microscopy/cell counts/biovolume?
- If not, is there any historical microscopy that overlaps the sensor period?
- Are phycobilin pigments measured, and by what method?
- What are the sample dates, depths and methods?
- What are the access terms?
- Does the public buoy dataset already cover this, or would additional data be needed?

## 4. Decision rules

### If WSA confirms species-level counts + biovolume/biomass + dates/depths/methods over multiple years

- Request metadata/data dictionary first.
- Assess temporal overlap with the buoy record.
- If overlap is adequate, update P3/P4 eligibility and return for human review.
- Do not assume the data are sufficient until the count-only audit is complete.

### If WSA has only chlorophyll or coarse cell counts

- Treat as contextual, not a primary microscopy endpoint.
- Do not restore P3/P4 to primary on this basis.

### If U Regina provides only chlorophyll/HPLC pigments

- Treat as contextual/functional-group data.
- Do not substitute for microscopy biovolume/biomass.
- It may help interpret sensor behaviour, but it is not the proposed endpoint.

### If City plant data are low taxonomic resolution

- Treat as contextual.
- Do not use as the primary independent endpoint.

### If U Saskatchewan has sensor data but no paired microscopy

- It cannot support P3/P4 as proposed.
- Buffalo Pound remains a two-season exploratory case.

## 5. What not to do

- Do not request full datasets before a metadata summary confirms a usable endpoint.
- Do not acquire data without explicit authorisation.
- Do not treat HPLC pigments, chlorophyll or sensor data as microscopy biovolume.
- Do not assume access will be free or unrestricted.
- Do not calculate usable n or correlations during this assessment.
- Do not change P3/P4 status without human approval.
