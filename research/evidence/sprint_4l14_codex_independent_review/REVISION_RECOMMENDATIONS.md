# Revision Recommendations

Issue a versioned 4L.14 addendum addressing these items before qualified human review:

1. **Biology record semantics:** define no cyanobacteria row, zero, non-detection and missing sample for each dataset. State whether the estimand is conditional on a reported cyanobacteria taxon.
2. **Buffalo Pound duplicates:** reconcile date × species repeated rows and `REP` fields against the paper/data deposit documentation. Select aggregation from evidence, not convenience. If irreducible, make P3/P4 explicitly exploratory with alternative endpoints clearly labeled, or drop them.
3. **Coverage:** remove the fixed 72/144 criterion until date-specific expected sampling and daily summary representativeness are established. Specify how unequal intervals, duplicate times and incomplete deployment affect the daily summary; report exposure duration, not just count.
4. **Fluorescence QC:** distinguish manufacturer operating range, data-paper ranges, baseline-corrected negative output, saturation, and QC flags. Treat unsupported thresholds as unresolved. Do not call flags true/false quality labels.
5. **Mendota station/biology support:** document station 1 versus blank and sample composition; clarify that sensor daily mean and 0–8m composite are spatial/depth mismatched.
6. **Question:** revise “across seasons and sensor eras” to reflect that only Mendota has distinct sensor eras; all season/year slices are descriptive and may be too sparse.
7. **Uncertainty:** retain LOYO/leave-date as influence checks. Remove optional bootstrap percentiles unless the statistical reviewer documents target, assumptions, sparse block rules and why it is useful.
8. **Prior art/biomass:** cite Painter paper methods directly, correct the full-text access statement, discuss the existing Buffalo Pound chlorophyll–biomass result and report remaining exact formula/replicate uncertainties.
9. **Scope and student story:** state plainly this project no longer tests EcoSentinel early warning; describe a useful negative result; no claims of validation, bloom detection, agreement or generalized sensor performance.
10. **Governance:** keep D-01–D-15 open; record whether new source-semantics questions are appended or incorporated with explicit traceability. D-15 stays unapproved.

After this addendum, qualified scientific, statistical, data-custodian and licensing reviewers should evaluate the revised proposal. A bounded count-only audit may then be separately considered; it must not be bundled with correlation authorization.
