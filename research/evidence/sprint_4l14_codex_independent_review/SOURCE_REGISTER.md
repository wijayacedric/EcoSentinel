## Source correction

4L.14 reports the Painter full paper was inaccessible. On this review date the publisher PDF was accessible at [RSC article PDF](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlepdf/2022/em/d2em00078d). Its methods describe microscopy, species-level identification, cell measurement and shape-specific geometry to convert counts to wet-weight biomass. It does not verify every detail of the exact local CSV formula or duplicate-row semantics. This review corrects the access claim without overstating method verification.
