# Competition Viability Assessment

This assessment is about likely project quality, not a prediction of a contest result.

| Dimension | Current assessment | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific merit | Moderate | Clear local measurement question; limited inference and unresolved sample rules |
| Originality | Low–moderate | Strong prior art; local synthesis only |
| Methodological rigor | Moderate potential, currently incomplete | Good governance and cautious language; arbitrary coverage and unresolved replicates/QC |
| Ecological significance | Moderate locally | Optical cyanobacteria monitoring matters; this design cannot guide alerts or exposure decisions |
| Student ownership | Not established by documents | Judges will expect the student to explain choices and limits without scripted jargon |
| Feasibility | High after scope control | Existing data; review/metadata reconciliation is manageable |
| Interpretability | Moderate-low | Seasonality, sensor eras, depth, unequal cadence and different endpoints complicate story |
| Potential results | Useful but uncertain | Negative finding may teach proxy limits, but cause may remain unidentified |
| Presentation potential | Moderate | Strong visual story possible later if authorized, with clear raw-data provenance and limits |

Compared with a genuinely strong student research entry, the current proposal lacks a sharply testable ecological mechanism, independent validation/calibration, clear evidence that missingness rules do not drive the result, and demonstrated student-led reasoning. Judges are likely to ask: Why does same-day mean anything if biology has no time? What does a negative RFU mean? Why 50% daily completeness? Are the repeated biology rows replicates? What does Painter et al. already show? Why does this answer EcoSentinel’s original early-warning question? How do you know two lakes differ because of anything other than the many confounders?

It could become award-competitive at a student competition if the student can: (1) accurately distinguish the new question from early warning; (2) resolve source row semantics and justify eligibility using original metadata; (3) show all outcomes without choosing a favorable channel; (4) explain why fluorescence is a context-sensitive proxy; (5) communicate uncertainty without pretending a descriptive range is a confidence interval; and (6) clearly own the research decisions and limitations. No new dataset is needed to address these weaknesses. Licence/competition compliance is a separate outstanding D-14 decision.

**Assessment:** a credible learning project is achievable; strong placement is possible but not presently supported by the protocol package alone. Do not inflate the project as a novel early-warning system.
