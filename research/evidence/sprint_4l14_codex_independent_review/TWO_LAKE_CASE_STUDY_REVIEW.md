# Two-Lake Case Study Review

The 4L.14 shift away from a controlled “depth-alignment” comparison is correct. The lakes cannot be pooled and one cannot validate the other. The case studies differ in too many linked dimensions for a cross-lake contrast to identify an effect of sensor, depth, sampling design or lake type.

| Dimension | Lake Mendota | Buffalo Pound Lake | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sensor | Turner Cyclops-era daily fluorescence; transition to YSI; device-specific RFU | YSI 6600 shallow probe; 10-minute records aggregated to days | No shared RFU calibration or direct scale comparison |
| Sensor depth | 0.5 m in the daily NTL package | nominal 0.8 m | Similar shallow point in broad terms, not identical measurement |
| Biology depth | 0–8 m tube composite | 0.8 m point sample | P1/P2 depth mismatch; P3/P4 nominal alignment |
| Biology period | 2006–2020, irregular long-term samples | two ice-free seasons, 2018–19 | Distinct temporal support, no generalization |
| Biology endpoint | Cyanobacteria biovolume, geometry/count derived | Supplied wet-weight biomass, geometry/count derived | Different quantities and scales; never pool |
| Taxonomy | NTL field/data revisions; broad Cyanophyta division | Painter species identification; Group/Phylum fields | Different taxonomic pipelines and names |
| Cadence | roughly fortnightly biological sampling, daily sensor summary | sparse field dates, continuous sensor | Different temporal mismatch and selection |
| Lake dynamics | deep, seasonally stratified dimictic lake | shallow, polymictic prairie reservoir | Different ecology and succession |
| QC/coverage | NTL flags; some undocumented values and missingness | YSI flag scheme, B7 interval; daily coverage variable | Exclusion populations not equivalent |

**Value of together:** modest cross-context demonstration that sensor–microscopy correspondence depends on local conditions. **Limit:** this is not a formal comparative design. A good result in both does not validate a universal proxy; different results do not identify why they differ. Treat each as a separate exploratory case report with one shared conceptual framework.

**Revisions:** Remove “across sensor eras” as a promised two-lake comparison; specify only Mendota has two sensor eras in the study period, while Buffalo Pound has one documented YSI era. If the aim is efficiency, P1/P2 alone could be a coherent single-lake study; adding P3/P4 increases scope and licensing/aggregation complexity without increasing independent evidence proportionally.
