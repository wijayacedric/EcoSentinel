## Verified/corroborated prior art relevant to candidate choice

- **Scheffer et al. 2009** (Nature): generic early-warning signals/critical slowing down. Foundational, not bloom-specific.
- **Dakos et al. 2012** (PLoS ONE): EWS methods on simulated data; performance depends on transition type/preprocessing.
- **Carpenter et al. 2011** (Science): whole-ecosystem early warning of regime shift; high-frequency lake monitoring.
- **Pace et al. 2017** (PNAS): early warnings and reversal of a cyanobacterial bloom in an experimental lake.
- **Rousso et al. 2020** (Water Research): systematic review of cyanobacteria forecasting models.
- **Recknagel et al. 2025** (Water Research): 5-day HAB forecasting, Lake Müggelsee, ML; independent review raised validation/leakage concerns (unverified in-session).
- **Shen & Chen 2026** (Water Research comment): validation/leakage/metrics concerns for Recknagel.
- **Fournier et al. 2024** (Water Research): LSTM cyanobacteria forecasting with incomplete spatio-temporal probe data; proxy target (phycocyanin), event-count/leakage cautions.
- **O'Brien et al. 2023** (Nature Communications): EWS have limited applicability to empirical lake data — important negative/cautionary prior art.

## Originality position

No originality claim is supportable at this stage. Matched false-alarm rates alone are not a novel contribution. A defensible contribution would require a clearly differentiated evaluation design against conventional/univariate baselines, transparent negative results, and independent biological endpoints — none of which is currently established under Gate 2.
