## CC BY 4.0 (buoy)

- Attribution required; commercial use permitted; redistribution permitted with attribution and licence notice.
- Compatible with competition submission, public presentation, source-code publication, and deployment **provided attribution is given**.

## CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (Painter biological data)

- **Attribution** required.
- **NonCommercial (NC):** use must not be primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation.
- **ShareAlike (SA):** adaptations/distributions must be under the same licence.
- Redistribution is permitted under these terms; modified datasets must carry the same licence and attribution.

## Potential compatibility concerns

1. **Competition submission:** rules vary; if the competition is non-commercial/academic, NC may be compatible, but this requires checking the competition terms. No legal determination made here.
2. **Public presentation:** allowed with attribution; if figures/data are adapted, SA may apply to the adapted material.
3. **Source-code publication:** code that reads/analyses the data may be distributed, but bundling the data or derived datasets can trigger SA; keep data separate and cite.
4. **Future deployment:** deployment in a commercial water-treatment/utility context may conflict with NC. A separate licence/permission would be required.
5. **Redistribution:** do not redistribute the Painter data without the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 notice and attribution; do not relicense it as CC BY.
6. **Mixed licences:** a combined product using CC BY buoy data and CC BY-NC-SA biological data should be treated as **NC-SA** for the combined/derived portion unless separated.

## Recommendation

Obtain human/licence review before competition submission, public release, code publication, or deployment. Keep raw data under their original licences; do not relicense. No legal conclusions beyond the documented terms.
