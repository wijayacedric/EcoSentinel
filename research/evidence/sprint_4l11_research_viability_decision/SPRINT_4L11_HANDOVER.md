## 1. Final decision

**CONTINUE — NARROWED RESEARCH QUESTION.**

The original early-warning question is **not currently testable** with the available evidence and is not realistically achievable within current constraints. It is preserved as a long-term scientific question, not pursued as a current confirmatory project.

The narrowed question is:

> How comparable are high-frequency surface sensor proxies (chlorophyll/phycocyanin fluorescence, DO, temperature) with independent microscopy-derived cyanobacteria biovolume in Lake Mendota and Buffalo Pound Lake, and how do agreement and data quality vary by season, sensor era, and depth alignment?

This is a descriptive measurement/comparability study, not an early-warning validation.

## 2. What was established

- **Original question viability:** biological validity, temporal validity, evaluation validity and statistical feasibility are **not satisfied**; measurement validity is **partially satisfied**.
- **Mendota:** retained for limited exploratory use; rejected for original early-warning validation. 402 biology dates; 128/139 same-day Turner proxies; 21/22 YSI; only 58/402 dates support a 7-variable Turner core; 2/402 support an 11-variable YSI core; 0.5 m vs 0–8 m.
- **Buffalo Pound:** exploratory only; 42 biology dates (2018–2019), 2 seasons, no non-bloom exposure, 1–16-day gaps, unresolved 2019-07-17 anomaly. Requests are reported sent by the user, but no reply/confirmation/data development is recorded; pending.
- **Other candidates:** Greifensee classifier-only; Western Lake Erie low-priority/inaccessible. No confirmatory-ready candidate.
- **Gate 2H:** synthetic software prototype only; 51/51 tests; no real labels, events, exposure or matched-FAR result.
- **Prior art:** high-frequency HAB early warning/forecasting (Recknagel 2025; Fournier 2024; Beal 2021/2022), algal-bloom EWS with TPR/FPR (Buelo 2022), empirical EWS limitations (Gsell 2016; O’Brien 2023), multivariate indicator limits (Weinans 2021), sensor-validation limits (Cotterill 2019; Levi 2025). Matched FAR is not novel.

## 4. Why not pause or pivot

- **Pause** is the fallback if the narrowed study cannot be made sufficiently original/defensible, or if competition deadlines require a positive early-warning result. It is not the primary recommendation because a defensible measurement study is feasible.
- **Pivot** is not recommended because no specific, evidence-based alternative direction has been identified; it would require new data/protocol and likely broad search/contact, which are not authorised.

## 5. Strongest counterargument

The narrowed measurement study may be too incremental for a top competition. Sensor-validation literature exists; Mendota lacks a depth profile; Buffalo Pound has only two seasons; cross-lake sensor/protocol differences confound the depth contrast. If the team needs a genuinely original early-warning result, current evidence cannot deliver it, and **PAUSE** would be more honest than overclaiming.
