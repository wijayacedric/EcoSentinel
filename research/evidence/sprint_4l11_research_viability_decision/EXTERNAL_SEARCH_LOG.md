## 3. Key findings

- **Original question not testable now.** 0 independently reconstructed bloom events; 0 defensible non-bloom intervals; no approved event definition; no matched-FAR denominator.
- **Buffalo Pound** has 42 biological dates over two seasons, no non-bloom season, interval-censored 1–16-day gaps, and no documented researcher reply/additional data. It is exploratory only.
- **Mendota** has 402 dates and 128/139 same-day Turner proxy dates, but 14-day biology, 0.5 m vs 0–8 m composite, Turner/YSI discontinuity, incomplete QC, and only 58/402 dates for a 7-variable Turner core. It is retained for limited exploratory use, rejected for validation.
- **Prior art is crowded.** High-frequency HAB early warning/forecasting exists (Recknagel 2025; Fournier 2024; Beal 2021/2022); algal-bloom EWS with TPR/FPR exists (Buelo 2022); empirical EWS often fail (Gsell 2016; O’Brien 2023); multivariate relationship indicators are scenario-dependent (Weinans 2021). Matched FAR is a quality requirement, not novelty.
- **Sensor proxies are not validated biomass.** Cotterill 2019 and Levi 2025 show weak/variable phycocyanin–biovolume relationships and calibration/composition/biofouling limits.
- **A narrowed measurement/comparability question** is feasible with existing data and is the strongest realistic route. It is not an early-warning validation.
