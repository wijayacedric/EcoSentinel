## 1. The three levels of claim

| Level | What it means | Current project asset | Scientific status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technically impressive demonstration** | Software pipeline, dashboards, synthetic tests, automation | Gate 2H prototype; 51/51 synthetic tests; data audit scripts | **Not a scientific result**; does not test the hypothesis |
| **Scientifically defensible investigation** | Clear question, appropriate data, pre-specified descriptive analysis, honest uncertainty | Mendota/Buffalo Pound audits; measurement data | **Possible** with a narrowed measurement question |
| **Genuinely original research contribution** | New validated finding or method that advances the field | None currently | **Not supported** by current evidence; requires new labels/validation |

Competition suitability does not prove scientific validity. A polished demo of an unvalidated early-warning system would be a technical demonstration, not evidence.

## 2. Competition fit for the narrowed measurement study

### SciFest@College

- **Fit:** Good. A clear question, independent microscopy, two-lake contrast, data-quality analysis and honest limitations fit the SciFest format.
- **Strengths:** Real data; independent biological reference; reproducible; negative/limited results are acceptable; strong methodology.
- **Weaknesses:** The question is measurement-focused, not a new early-warning system; judges may see it as incremental relative to sensor-validation literature.
- **Readiness:** High if a strict descriptive protocol is written; no new data access needed.
- **Risk:** Overclaiming “sensor validation” when the study only tests surface-vs-composite comparability.

### Earth Prize

- **Fit:** Moderate/low. Earth Prize tends to favor environmental solutions, innovation and impact. A measurement/feasibility study can inform monitoring but is not a deployable solution.
- **Strengths:** Addresses a real environmental monitoring problem; honest about limits.
- **Weaknesses:** No new intervention/tool; competition narrative may be less compelling.
- **Readiness:** Medium; would need a strong “why this matters for monitoring” framing.

### GENIUS Olympiad

- **Fit:** Good. Science/ecology category values a clear hypothesis, data, analysis and conclusions. A measurement/comparability study fits.
- **Strengths:** Independent biology, rigorous data audit, two-lake contrast, clear limitations.
- **Weaknesses:** Originality moderate; results may be negative/limited.
- **Readiness:** High with a concise protocol and analysis.

### ISEF / EUCYS pathways

- **Fit:** Potentially, subject to eligibility and local affiliation.
- **Strengths:** Strong data provenance and measurement focus.
- **Weaknesses:** Originality and “wow” factor may be lower than a validated early-warning system; eligibility unknown.
- **Readiness:** Cannot be assessed without eligibility confirmation; do not assume.

## 4. What the narrowed study can and cannot deliver

**Can deliver:**
- a clear two-lake descriptive comparison of sensor proxies and microscopy biovolume;
- quantification of same-day agreement by season, sensor era and depth alignment;
- a documented negative/limited result: surface RFU proxies are not interchangeable with depth-integrated biovolume;
- a rigorous measurement-limitations contribution.

**Cannot deliver:**
- a validated early-warning system;
- bloom/non-bloom labels or event intervals;
- a matched-FAR comparison;
- a claim that relational changes precede blooms;
- cross-lake causal inference about depth.

## 5. Competition strategy

- Present the project as a **measurement/comparability and evidence-feasibility study**, not as an early-warning validation.
- Lead with the scientific question and the independent biological reference.
- Show the data audit, the depth/era/QC limitations, and the negative/limited result.
- Explicitly state what would be required to test the original early-warning question.
- Do not use a synthetic prototype or a polished dashboard as a substitute for empirical validation.
- Do not claim novelty from matched FAR, a new algorithm, or the Mendota/Buffalo Pound datasets alone.

## 6. Bottom line

The narrowed measurement study is **practically feasible** and **scientifically defensible**, with **moderate competition potential**. It is not a genuinely original early-warning contribution. If the team requires a positive early-warning flagship for a specific competition, the honest answer is that the current evidence cannot support it; a pause or a different, explicitly approved project would be more defensible than overclaiming.
