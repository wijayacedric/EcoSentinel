## Manufacturer evidence (inspected)

- **YSI EXO2:** multiparameter sonde with a central antifouling wiper; Total Algae chlorophyll/phycocyanin sensors; typical ranges 0–400 µg/L Chl and 0–100 µg/L PC / 0–100 RFU; anti-fouling wiper and SmartQC calibration support. Manufacturer performance is typical, not a guarantee in natural water.
- **Turner Designs Cyclops-7F:** submersible fluorometer; calibration with liquid dye standards; discontinued 2025; biofouling and calibration are known operational issues. The 4L.1 sensor list identifies Turner Cyclops as the 2006–2019 chlorophyll/phycocyanin sensor.
- **EML:** daily values are averages of 1-minute observations; flags C/E/H/G; G only for YSI chlorophyll/phycocyanin and PAR-below; no calibration/serial/fouling logs.

## What cannot be assessed

- Actual calibration dates/coefficients, drift, biofouling episodes, maintenance, detection limits, zero/invalid readings, or flag distributions.
- Whether the 2019 Turner→YSI transition is cross-calibrated.
- Whether daily averaging masks diel/event-scale variation.

## Interpretation

Chlorophyll and phycocyanin are **sensor proxies** (RFU), not calibrated biomass or cell counts. They require independent microscopy validation. Temperature/DO/pH/conductance are more directly interpretable but still need QC and depth context. Across-era values are not automatically comparable.

No measurement-quality claim is made beyond the documentation above.
