# Buffalo Pound Researcher Data Requirements

**Date:** 2026-11-23T23:27:42.541908+00:00  
**Gate 2:** PAUSED / NOT APPROVED — Option C (Exploratory Only)

Purpose: precise list of additional evidence that could materially change Buffalo Pound's confirmatory feasibility. No numerical minimums are invented; requirements are framed as design capabilities.

## Priority 1 — Additional independent biological sampling years

- **Why:** 2018-2019 provide only two seasonal cycles; multi-year coverage is needed for held-out temporal validation, recurrence assessment, and stable false-alarm rates.
- **Addresses:** Sprint 4F limitation "two seasons/42 dates".
- **Minimum evidence:** dates, station, depth, methods, taxonomic list, raw counts/biomass, QC flags, detection limits, licence.
- **Could change feasibility:** **Yes, substantially**, if methods are comparable and enough years exist to hold out entire years.

## Priority 2 — Additional microscopy/taxonomic observations

- **Why:** The verified endpoint is species-level microscopy; adding years/occasions of the same measurement type strengthens independence and taxonomic consistency.
- **Addresses:** small effective N; 2018 vs 2019 taxonomy differences.
- **Minimum evidence:** raw count sheets/slides, counting replicates, taxon names/codes, biovolume/biomass calculations, identification QA, taxonomy updates.
- **Could change feasibility:** **Yes**, especially if comparable methods span multiple years.

## Priority 3 — Biological sampling outside bloom seasons

- **Why:** Non-bloom exposure cannot be defensibly established from bloom-season-only samples; spring/fall/winter or low-biomass periods are needed.
- **Addresses:** "no defensible non-bloom exposure".
- **Minimum evidence:** dates/times, station/depth, counts/biomass, methods, and evidence that sampling was scheduled rather than triggered by blooms.
- **Could change feasibility:** **Yes, essential** for any event/non-event evaluation.

## Priority 4 — Higher-frequency biological observations

- **Why:** Median 4-8 d cadence and no sample times limit onset/lead-time resolution; event-triggered or daily sampling would reduce interval censoring.
- **Addresses:** "cadence too coarse".
- **Minimum evidence:** sample timestamps, event-trigger logs, daily/rapid-response counts, preserved samples.
- **Could change feasibility:** **Potentially**, but only if independent and consistently collected; otherwise descriptive only.

## Priority 5 — Independent bloom and non-bloom evidence

- **Why:** A defensible event label requires independent evidence (e.g., microscopy/biomass thresholds, toxin data, visual/remote sensing) and documented adjudication.
- **Addresses:** no event/non-event design.
- **Minimum evidence:** event definition, dates, evidence type, uncertainty, station/depth, and non-bloom intervals.
- **Could change feasibility:** **Yes**, but labels/design are Gate 2 work and are not authorised now.

## Priority 6 — Historical datasets not in public repositories

- **Why:** Pre-2018 or 2020+ microscopy may exist in lab records/archives and could extend coverage.
- **Addresses:** two-season limitation.
- **Minimum evidence:** provenance, methods, dates, counts, taxonomy, QC, licence.
- **Could change feasibility:** **Yes**, if comparable and complete.

## Priority 7 — Methodology, station, depth and QC documentation

- **Why:** Needed to harmonise 2018/2019, align to buoy depths, interpret flags/sensor changes, and assess detection limits.
- **Addresses:** schema/taxonomy/replicate/QC/alignment uncertainties.
- **Minimum evidence:** sampling protocols, station coordinates, sample times, depth integration, replicate design, calibration/fouling logs, data dictionaries.
- **Could change feasibility:** **Yes, enabling** — without it, even additional data may be unusable.

## What would NOT change feasibility

- Additional derived matrices/group biomass from the same counts.
- Additional YSI profiles without new biological occasions.
- More sensor records from the same two seasons.
- Re-analysis of the same 42 dates.

## Request framing (do not send)

Ask for a metadata-first inventory (dates, variables, methods, QC, licence) before any data transfer. Do not request credentials or restricted data; do not contact anyone in this sprint.
