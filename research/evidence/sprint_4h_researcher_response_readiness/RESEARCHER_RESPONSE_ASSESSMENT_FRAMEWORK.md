# Researcher Response Assessment Framework

**Date:** 2026-11-23T23:27:42.541908+00:00  
**Gate 2:** PAUSED / NOT APPROVED — Option C (Exploratory Only)

## Purpose

Assess pending University of Saskatchewan/University of Regina responses consistently. No contact is made in this sprint.

## Response quality criteria

For any offered dataset, record: provenance/DOI, licence, measurement method, taxonomic resolution, raw counts/biomass, dates/times, station/depth, sampling design (scheduled vs event-triggered), QC/detection limits, missingness, independence from sensors, and comparability with 2018/2019.

## Scenarios and actions

### A. Additional compatible biological data exist
1. Request a metadata-first inventory (no data transfer yet).
2. Assess licence, methods, dates, taxonomy, counts/biomass, QC, station/depth.
3. If compatible: plan selective acquisition with SHA256/provenance; update Gate 2 tracker.
4. Do not label/modelling; do not assume confirmatory feasibility until file-level audit.

### B. Additional data exist but insufficient temporal coverage
1. Document the gap precisely (years/seasons/dates).
2. Consider exploratory use only if independently useful.
3. Do not force confirmatory evaluation; keep Buffalo Pound pending/exploratory.

### C. Researchers identify alternative datasets
1. Screen against endpoint quality, temporal coverage, HF predictors, alignment, licence.
2. Perform metadata audit before acquisition.
3. Do not switch candidates automatically; update comparison matrix.

### D. Methodological clarification only
1. Update methods/QC/taxonomy documentation.
2. Re-assess comparability of 2018/2019 and alignment.
3. Feasibility changes only if the clarification resolves a material limitation (e.g., non-bloom sampling, replicate design), not merely description.

### E. No additional compatible data exist
1. Record negative response.
2. Buffalo Pound remains exploratory-only; do not reject the existing verified data.
3. Pivot to Mendota authorised access or other multi-year independent biology.

### F. No response received
1. Do not repeatedly contact.
2. Continue independent tasks (Mendota metadata/access, candidate screening).
3. Keep Buffalo Pound pending; set an internal review checkpoint without inventing a scientific deadline.

## Decision rule

Only additional **independent biological observations across additional years and non-bloom periods**, with documented methods/QC/licence, can move Buffalo Pound toward confirmatory readiness. Derived files, same-season sensor records, or methodological notes alone do not.
