## Inaccessible / not fully inspected

- No original microscope slides, count sheets, lab calculation spreadsheet, version history/change log beyond EML, or source-owner record for suspicious rows was available.
- ScienceDirect and Taylor & Francis blocked direct article page retrieval (HTTP 403); Mendota forecast details were inspected in search-indexed full text/metadata, not the article PDF. O'Brien et al. Nature Communications full PDF was inspected, including Table 2 and Methods/Discussion.
- NTL's current protocol page says protocols are being revised and access to some updated procedures requires contacting the program. No contact was made. The publicly accessible archived 2023 PDF is not established as the controlling protocol for all years of dataset 88.31.
- Some taxonomy authority pages were not consistently accessible or disagree. The 2024 checklist is an authoritative scholarly source but geographically scoped; it was not used to overwrite the project crosswalk.
- This audit does not claim to have independently re-reviewed every citation in all 4L.5 source trails or completed a comprehensive systematic literature review. Existing-use statements remain limited to inspected sources/project evidence.
