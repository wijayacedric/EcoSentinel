## Documented sampling design

- Four southern Wisconsin lakes: **Mendota, Monona, Wingra, Fish**.
- Mendota: **composite whole-water 0–8 m** using an integrated tube sampler at the deep hole.
- Other lakes: 0–2 m composite (1-m interval Kemmerer samples pooled).
- Preserved in **glutaraldehyde**; permanent slide mounts (3 per sample) prepared by **PhycoTech**; archived at UW–Madison Zoology Museum.
- Identification: inverted microscope, **Utermöhl technique**, species level; natural-unit, cell and biovolume densities.
- Sampling frequency: biweekly late Mar/Apr–early Sep, 4-weekly to late Nov, usually one winter sample.
